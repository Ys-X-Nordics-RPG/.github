## What Is Ys X: Nordics?

Ys X: Nordics is an action role-playing game developed by Nihon Falcom. The adventure follows Adol Christin and Karja Balta across the Obelia Gulf, where seafaring communities, mysterious forces, and dangerous enemies shape their journey.

The game combines real-time combat, island exploration, naval travel, character development, and a story influenced by Norse maritime themes. Its central combat feature allows the two main characters to fight separately or work together through coordinated actions.

Players sail between locations, discover new areas, engage hostile forces, and uncover more of the world through quests and story events.
